--- strlen scaling (results_strlen.txt) ---

Shared floor 0.2–0.8µs: every cmd is Cvar_Get + Cvar_Set2. Both walk cvar_vars
(next +0x1C) with 2-byte strcmp, then CopyString/Z_Free. Hundreds of cvars beat
strlen of a 256 blob. Engine: Cvar_Get 0x20021ae0, Cvar_Set → Cvar_Set2 0x20021d70.
